# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

Update the numbers to match your own run (the dashboard shows them).

**The problem.** "612 of 2,000 locations lose money, and margin is about 5%. Cutting vendor prices treats every site the same, but they lose money for different reasons."

**One site.** Point to the detail card below the map. "This site's contract assumes 3 hours a visit. Geofencing shows 4.3 on site, and the vendor bills 5.3. The data shows exactly why it loses money, and which fixes apply."

**Causes.** "Each site's gap to a 25% margin splits into four causes. Travel and low density is the biggest. I planted known problems in the synthetic data, and the diagnosis finds most of them."

**Routes.** "In New York today, 7 vendors crisscross the city. K-Means groups sites into territories, and 2-opt builds each route from OpenStreetMap drive times, the same method as my RedEye project. That's 63% less driving."

**Plan.** Move a slider, then click "With the plan" at the top. "With conservative assumptions, losing sites drop from 612 to 138, and margin rises from about 5% to 17%. Every assumption is in one config file."

**First step.** "Start with the location P&L and diagnosis, since it changes nothing for customers. Then a 90-day territory pilot in one metro against a control metro."
